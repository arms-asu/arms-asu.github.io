How we build

Every project runs three lanes.

Each build, whether it is a club robot or a company project, pulls from the same bench of skills.

Software

Robot control, perception, and autonomy code — ROS 2, embedded firmware, and the glue that makes hardware listen.

Hardware

Robot arms, mobile bases, and custom electronics — CAD, machining, and fabrication on campus research equipment.

Simulation

Physics-accurate testing before anything is built — including NVIDIA Isaac Sim — so hardware time goes further.

Industry projects

Contract-style projects with companies.

We take on projects with companies the way capstone teams do: you bring a real problem, and we work with you to build a genuine solution. Our members work across robotics software, hardware, and simulation, with access to research equipment on campus.

Our first industry collaborations are in discussion now. This page will showcase each project as it lands.
