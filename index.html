Who we are

A student-run crew turning ideas into hardware.

ARMS brings together builders, coders, and the robot-curious at Arizona State University's Polytechnic campus. Whether you've never touched a soldering iron or you've machined your own parts, there's a bench here for you.

We run hands-on projects, host workshops, and field competition builds through the school year.

What we do

Three lanes, one club.

Build

Robot arms, mobile bases, electronics, CAD, machining and fabrication — real hardware, designed and assembled by members.

Compete

Intercollegiate robotics competitions and hackathons. Design reviews, crunch weeks, and the walk onto the competition floor.

Teach

Workshops on robotics, electronics, and manufacturing — from first solder joint to full system integration. Beginners welcome.

Connect

Come build with us.

Follow the builds, catch workshop announcements, or just come say hi.
